- **Scope and mode:** Replace the projects dashboard visual world and interaction hierarchy; Operate mode. Settings inherit the same system without changing their functionality.
- **Audience and job:** A solo developer or home-lab owner keeps Porto open while managing one machine. They must see running, unhealthy, and attention states within seconds, then act without remembering commands, ports, or process IDs.
- **Direction:** Broadcast patchbay and electronics service-bench language. Projects are compact channels with engraved labels, status lamps, routes, and a service drawer. The memorable moment is the fleet reading as one trustworthy control surface rather than a collection of cards.
- **Interaction:** Health summary and filters lead. Each project has immediate run controls and an expandable inline drawer for branches, runtime detail, commands, logs, and destructive or uncommon actions. Avoid oversized cards, empty space, decorative motion, and hidden state.
- **Constraints:** Preserve all current project, log, branch, instance, setup, Sendbox, cleanup, and settings behavior. Remain responsive, keyboard accessible, readable with large project collections, and explicit around failures and destructive actions.
