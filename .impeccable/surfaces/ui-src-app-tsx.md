- **Scope and mode:** Shared desktop/web operations shell; Operate mode. `localhost-ing` replaces the old projects dashboard while every Docker, Kubernetes, VM, activity, and settings surface inherits the same system.
- **Audience and job:** A solo developer or home-lab owner keeps Porto open while managing one machine. They must see running, unhealthy, and attention states within seconds, then act without remembering commands, ports, process IDs, contexts, or resource names.
- **Direction:** Broadcast patchbay and electronics service-bench language. A fixed graphite source rail feeds fused fleet/control instruments, dense ranked inventories, and a docked inspector. The memorable moment is native apps, containers, clusters, and VMs reading as one trustworthy control desk.
- **Interaction:** Navigation and health lead. Common actions stay on inventory rows; selection opens detail, logs, terminals, files, metrics, routing, and uncommon/destructive actions in the adjacent inspector. At narrow widths the rail becomes off-canvas and the inspector becomes a full-screen detail surface.
- **Constraints:** Preserve project, log, branch, instance, setup, Sendbox, cleanup, and settings behavior. Optional runtimes default off and must degrade clearly when unavailable. Remain responsive, keyboard accessible, readable with large inventories, and explicit around failures and persistent-data deletion.
